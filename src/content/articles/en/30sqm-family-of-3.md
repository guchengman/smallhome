## Project Background

This is an old apartment in a tier-1 city center, just 30sqm with 2.8m ceiling height, needing to accommodate a family of three. The designer faced the challenge: how to fit two bedrooms and a living area into an extremely compact space while maintaining comfort.

## Space Planning Strategy

The designer adopted a **vertical zoning** strategy, fully utilizing the 2.8m ceiling height to divide the space into three functional layers.

### Ground Level (0-60cm)

- Full tatami platform, storage underneath equals ~15 large suitcases
- Built-in shoe cabinet at the entrance, holds 30 pairs
- Sunk sink in kitchen area

### Mid Level (60-180cm)

- Multi-function zone: living + dining + study
- Folding dining table, fully retracts into wall
- Storage drawers under sofa
- Mobile desk

### Upper Level (180cm+)

- Loft-style master bedroom and children's room
- Master: 1.5m bed + nightstand
- Children's: 1.2m bed + desk
- Floor-to-ceiling cabinets

## Key Design Highlights

### Folding Dining Table

A custom table that tucks completely into the wall. When extended, it comfortably seats 4 with wall-mounted brackets. When not in use, the wall shows decorative art instead.

**Key Specs**:
- Extended: 120cm × 70cm
- Storage depth: only 8cm (within wall)
- Load capacity: 80kg

### Tatami Multi-function Zone

Floor raised 40cm for tatami platform with compartmentalized storage underneath. By day: living room. By night: private bedroom via sliding partition doors.

### Floor-to-Ceiling Storage

Using the 2.8m ceiling height, a full-wall cabinet system in the hallway corridor. Only 35cm deep but incredibly efficient.

## Summary

Living as a family of 3 in 30sqm is not impossible — the key is meticulous design of every centimeter. This case proves small homes can deliver high-quality living when you leverage vertical space, custom multi-functional furniture, and smart storage planning.
