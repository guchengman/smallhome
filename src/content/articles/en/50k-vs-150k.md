## What 50K RMB Gets You

A 50K budget covers basic renovation or partial updates. Core principle: light on hard finishes, invest in furnishings.

### Budget Allocation

- Electrical: minimal changes, keep existing wiring
- Flooring: laminate (80-120 RMB/sqm)
- Walls: latex paint, no decorative moldings
- Cabinetry: particle board with quartz countertop
- Bathroom: domestic entry-level brands

### Trade-offs

- Lower material quality, watch for VOCs
- No design fee budget
- Simple storage systems
- Some DIY work needed

## What 150K RMB Gets You

A 150K budget delivers quality finishes with noticeably better materials and design.

### Budget Allocation

- Electrical: full rewiring, smart home prep
- Flooring: engineered wood (200-350 RMB/sqm)
- Walls: paint + accent wallpaper/feature wall
- Cabinetry: multi-ply wood with branded hardware
- Bathroom: top domestic or entry-level imported

### Advantages

- Higher环保 ratings (E0 boards, imported paints)
- Independent designer possible
- Custom cabinetry maximizes storage
- Layered lighting design

## Key Differences

| Item | 50K Plan | 150K Plan |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Flooring | Laminate | Engineered wood |
| Cabinets | Particle board | Multi-ply wood |
| Bathroom | Basic domestic | Mid-range branded |
| Design | None | Independent designer |
| Storage | Ready-made furniture | Full custom |
| Contractor | Foreman | Brand company |

## Advice

If your budget falls between 50-100K, invest most in hidden work (electrical, waterproofing). Other elements can be upgraded later.
