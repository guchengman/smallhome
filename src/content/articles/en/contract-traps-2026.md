## Trap 1: Vague Pricing

"Billed by actual work" is the biggest trap. Without a fixed price, final bills can exceed quotes by 50%+.

**Solution**: Demand a fixed-price contract or itemize every line with unit price and estimated quantity.

## Trap 2: Missing Items

Contractors deliberately omit items to keep the initial quote low, then add them back later.

**Common omissions**: Debris removal, material handling, cleaning hardware, angle valves, floor drains.

## Trap 3: Open-Ended Electrical Work

"We'll charge by actual meter length" is classic. Quoted as "maybe 5-6K," billed as "12K."

**Solution**: Request a flat fee for electrical/plumbing, or cap overruns at 10% of estimate.

## Trap 4: Brand Names Without Models

"Brand X pipes" without specifying the model line means you get the cheapest line they make.

**Solution**: Contracts must specify brand, model, specification, and color for all materials.

## Trap 5: Unbalanced Payment Schedule

Paying 60%+ before work starts leaves you with no leverage.

**Recommended split**: 30% signing → 30% rough-in → 30% final → 10% after 30-day move-in.

## Trap 6: Vague Timeline

"X days" without start/end dates or penalty clauses.

**Solution**: Specify start and completion dates, with daily penalty (recommend 0.1%+ of total).

## Trap 7: Subcontracting

The company takes a cut and subcontracts to unlicensed crews.

**Solution**: Add "no subcontracting without approval; penalty = 30% of total contract value."

## Trap 8: Unclear Warranty

"2-year warranty" without specifying what's covered.

**Solution**: 5 years for waterproofing, 5 years for concealed work, 2 years for general — with clear scope.

## Trap 9: Verbal Promises

Sales promises free upgrades or additions, then "forgets" after signing.

**Solution**: Every promise must be written into the contract or appendix.

## Trap 10: Unfair Clauses

"Owner sign-off = acceptance" prevents third-party inspection. "Change orders capped at 5%" lets them offload omissions onto you.

**Solution**: Refuse unfair terms. Walk away if they won't negotiate.
